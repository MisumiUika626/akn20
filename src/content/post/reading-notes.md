:::note
This English-only sample demonstrates how an article without a translation behaves. It does not summarize an actual paper.
:::

When reading a chapter or paper, record four things:

1. The question the author is trying to answer.
2. The assumption the argument depends on.
3. A small example you can check independently.
4. The point you still cannot explain.

A note can stay unfinished. An explicit question is more useful than a confident sentence without evidence.
